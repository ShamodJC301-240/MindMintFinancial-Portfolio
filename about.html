Our Story

Banking built for New Yorkers.

The Mint was founded on a simple belief: banking should be transparent, accessible, and genuinely helpful — not confusing, expensive, or impersonal.

Our Mission

Money should work for everyone.

We started The Mint because too many New Yorkers were paying unnecessary fees, struggling to understand their finances, and being underserved by traditional banks that prioritized profit over people.

Our mission is to combine the trust and stability of a community bank with the tools and transparency of a modern fintech — all under one roof, across five NYC neighborhoods.
